# Industrial login media

The six scenes are illustrative images generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, not records of real company projects. The reference supplied by the user guided the subjects, without copying its card text. No real company documents or secrets are included.

The images were composed into `operations-film.mp4` with FFmpeg: six scenes, one-second crossfades, 1280 × 720, H.264, 24 fps, no audio, fast-start playback. The closing transition returns to the opening scene. `operations-poster.jpg` is the first frame. The login client honors reduced motion, pauses on sign-in and when the tab becomes hidden, and provides a pause/play control.
